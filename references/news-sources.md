## Sourcing rules

- **Primary source over aggregator.** Company blog / filing / data release beats a hot take about it.
- **Verify anon takes before repeating them** — especially anything with a stock angle.
- **Separate signal from noise.** A loud headline is not a confirmed event; flag confidence level.
- **Recency matters most** for fast-moving topics — lead with the last 24–48h.
- **Note when a theme has an equity angle** and hand off to `sector-rotation-stock-hunter`
  rather than screening tickers inside a digest skill.
